# 03-02 Member payment and proof upload

Status: planned. Depends on 03-01. Owners: PAY-01, PAY-02, PAY-03, PAY-07 (member side), UX-01, UX-06, UX-08, UX-09; preserves AUTH-05.

User story: from my booking I see exactly what to pay and how, pay with QRIS on this phone or by transfer, send the proof, and know truthfully whether it arrived; if it was rejected I can fix it in the time left.

- [ ] `GET /api/customer/bookings/:id/payment` and `POST /api/customer/bookings/:id/proofs` (multipart, `submissionId` replay, deadline checked on complete receipt, transition to `awaiting_verification` in one transaction with file commit/rollback). OpenAPI `03-02-OPENAPI.yaml`.
- [ ] Club payment content (QRIS image, merchant name/NMID, bank, account, holder) in club content with the sample/placeholder values and the "Contoh QR, belum bisa dipakai bayar" label.
- [ ] Pay screen `/bookings/:id/pay`:
  - Phone: the booking line, "Bayar sebelum" with countdown, QRIS/Transfer switch (sliding marker), large QR with "Simpan QR", bank rows with Salin, exact total with Salin and the "ketik nominal ini persis" note, the three numbered steps, the upload area (choose or take photo; up to 3; thumbnails; remove), "Kirim bukti bayar".
  - Desktop: board D8 layout.
- [ ] Upload states: per-file validation as field errors; sending shows progress text; interrupted/failed shows a notice with Coba lagi keeping files; success is the board's dedicated "Bukti terkirim" screen (no toast); under 10 minutes left a warning notice; deadline passed shows the notice with a WhatsApp link and no upload.
- [ ] Entry points: "Bayar" / "Cara bayar" on Booking kamu cards, the booking detail page and the desktop panel, and from the booking confirmation's payment box (replace its placeholder text). Returning to the pay screen restores nothing typed (only files), and the booking is always reachable from Booking kamu (UX-08).
- [ ] Booking kamu and detail show the new states: Menunggu dicek (no countdown; "Slot kamu aman sampai admin selesai cek"), Perlu diperbaiki (reason, time left, "Kirim bukti baru"), Lunas.
- [ ] Copy: casual member voice; G1 wording (what happened, then what to do).
- [ ] Tests: submit before/at/after deadline; replay of the same `submissionId`; a different booking's owner; invalid/oversize/disguised files; rollback leaves no file; needs_correction resubmission keeps the original deadline; UI states in unit-free browser probes at 390/1024/1440.

Acceptance: a member can go from Booking kamu to a submitted proof on a phone without sideways scrolling or hidden actions; no success is shown unless the server stored the proof; the deadline never moves.
