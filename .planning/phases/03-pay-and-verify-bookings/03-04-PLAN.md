# 03-04 Payments received outside the app

Status: planned. Depends on 03-03. Owner: OPER-08.

User story: when someone pays cash at the court or transfers without uploading proof, I record it and the booking is paid.

- [ ] `POST /api/admin/bookings/:id/payments` (method, amount, paid on, note, `requestId` replay, `version`); confirms the booking; revives an expired booking only if the admission capacity check still admits it, else refuses with a clear code. OpenAPI `03-04-OPENAPI.yaml`.
- [ ] "Catat pembayaran" on the booking (phone page, desktop drawer): method chips, amount prefilled with the total (editable, difference shown as a note line), date (today by default, the custom date field), optional note; field errors with shake; toast "Pembayaran tercatat."; the payment appears in the booking's payment history with who recorded it.
- [ ] Booking masuk and rosters show Lunas; Rekap counts it (03-05).
- [ ] Tests: record on each state; expired revival with and without capacity; replay; amount differences; cancelled session refused.

Acceptance: an admin can record an outside payment from a phone in under a minute and the booking reads Lunas everywhere.
