# 04-01 Cancelled status, booking cancel and refunds due

Status: planned. Depends on accepted Phase 3. Owners: OPER-11, CANC-08 (refund due), BOOK-10 (Dibatalkan).

User story: a member messages that two of their four players can't come; from my phone I remove those two, see the refund it creates, and the member's booking shows the new count and the refund on its way.

## Acceptance

From a phone at 390, an admin cancels a booking whole and in part; places return to the schedule immediately; the refund due is visible on the booking; the member sees Dibatalkan or the reduced booking with the refund line, in ID and EN.
