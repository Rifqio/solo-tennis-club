# 04-03 Edit booking information and move a booking

Status: planned. Depends on 04-01. Owners: OPER-09, OPER-10.

User story: a member asks on WhatsApp to switch to Thursday and fix a player's name; I move the booking in two taps, the app tells me it fits, and anything paid stays paid.

## Tasks

- [ ] `server/features/admin/bookings/booking-edit.service.ts`: booker name, contact phone (normalised like admission), participant names; names only after session start; a verified member's account is not changed; change event with before/after.
- [ ] `server/features/admin/bookings/booking-move.service.ts`: move options (scheduled, not started, admin booking cutoff, `fits` by the admission capacity check); move in one transaction: capacity check on the destination through `booking-admission`, `sessionId` change, price rules (unpaid takes destination price, keeps `originalDeadline`; paid cheaper creates a `price_difference` refund; paid dearer returns `owedDifferenceIdr`), status kept, change event. A failed check changes nothing (`CAPACITY_UNAVAILABLE`, `SAME_SESSION`, `SESSION_STARTED`). Routes and OpenAPI `04-03-OPENAPI.yaml`.
- [ ] Booking detail actions: Ubah opens the edit form (phone page, desktop drawer section) with the existing field components; Pindah sesi opens a picker grouped by day (DateStrip pattern) listing classes with places left and price, disabled when it does not fit, then a confirm naming the price effect ("Harga sesi ini Rp 120.000. Selisih Rp 30.000 dicatat sebagai refund." / "Selisih Rp 20.000 belum dibayar; catat di catatan pembayaran?").
- [ ] Riwayat perubahan on the booking detail (collapsed list: what changed, who, when).
- [ ] After a move: toast and the ready WhatsApp message "Booking kamu dipindah ke {session}".
- [ ] Member: the booking shows its new session; an unpaid one keeps its countdown.

## Tests

- Integration `tests/integration/booking-edit-move.test.ts`: edit fields and limits after start; move succeeds and frees the old places while taking the new ones (occupancy on both sessions); destination full leaves the booking exactly as it was; unpaid takes the new price and keeps the deadline; paid cheaper creates the refund; paid dearer returns the difference without a refund; status kept for every status; cancelled or expired bookings cannot move; version conflict; replay; two admins moving into the last places race (one wins).

## Acceptance

From a phone, an admin edits names and moves a booking; capacity on both sessions is right afterwards; a full destination is refused without changing anything; money effects match 04-DECISIONS §6.
