# 04-04 Refund completion, late money and Rekap

Status: planned. Depends on 04-02 and 04-03. Owners: CANC-09, CANC-08 (late money), BOOK-10 (refund lines).

User story: at the end of the week I open Refund, return the money by transfer, mark each one done, and Rekap shows what we really earned.

## Tasks

- [ ] `server/features/admin/bookings/refund.service.ts` (or `server/features/admin/refunds/` if it grows): complete (method, club date defaulting to today, note), waive (note required), `REFUND_NOT_DUE` for anything not due; late money on a cancelled booking, or an expired one whose places are gone, records a `PaymentRecord` and a `late_payment` refund in one transaction. Routes `POST /api/admin/refunds/:id/complete|waive`, `POST /api/admin/bookings/:id/late-payments`. OpenAPI `04-04-OPENAPI.yaml`.
- [ ] Phase 3's record-payment keeps reviving an `expired` booking when its places are free (decision 11). When the capacity check refuses it, the detail offers "Catat uang masuk" instead of the error; record-payment refuses a `cancelled` booking.
- [ ] Overpayment: a booking whose `paymentNote` or recorded amounts exceed the total offers "Jadikan refund" (`overpayment`).
- [ ] Booking masuk: a Refund filter after Perlu dicek with its count, oldest first, showing amount, reason and "Cek dulu" when flagged; the refund card on the detail with Tandai sudah dikembalikan (sheet/dialog: Transfer · Tunai · QRIS, date, note) and Tidak jadi (note); a short G1 undo after marking done.
- [ ] Rekap (`recap.service.ts`, `recap-export.ts`, `AdminRecapPage`): revenue minus completed refunds in the session's month; a "Refund belum dikembalikan" tile (sum and count); the Excel export gains a Refund sheet.
- [ ] Member: "Refund Rp X sedang diproses" while due, "Refund Rp X sudah dikembalikan ({date})" once completed; waived refunds are not shown.

## Tests

- Integration `tests/integration/refunds.test.ts`: complete and waive transitions, note required to waive, not-due refused, replay; late money creates payment and refund and never changes the booking status; record-payment refuses an expired booking; overpayment refund; Refund filter order and count; Rekap revenue with completed and due refunds across months; export contains the Refund sheet; member sees only their own refund lines.

## Acceptance

An admin clears the Refund list on a phone; Rekap revenue and the refund tile match the records; members see each refund's state.
